All six original Lab 11 beverages and their exact prices are preserved: Espresso (Rs. 250), Cappuccino (Rs. 350), Latte (Rs. 300), Milk Tea (Rs. 150), Green Tea (Rs. 180), and Black Tea (Rs. 120).
