Our Story & Identity

A Cozy Sanctuary Built Around Simple Quality

My Cafe began with a clear vision: serve honest, comforting beverages—from a bold shot of Espresso at Rs. 250 to a soothing cup of Black Tea at Rs. 120—without unnecessary complexity.

Every cup is prepared to order using freshly ground beans, calibrated water temperatures, and traditional steeping methods. Whether you are stopping by for a morning Cappuccino before classes or settling into a corner booth for afternoon study, My Cafe offers consistent quality and calm hospitality.
